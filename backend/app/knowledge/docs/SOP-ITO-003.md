## 1 Purpose
Computerised systems are periodically evaluated to confirm that they remain in a validated state and
compliant with GMP (EU GMP Annex 11 §11).

## 2 Frequency
The review interval is set per system based on risk and is shown in the Syntra system inventory:
normally 24 months for validated GxP systems, and 12 months for new or high-risk interfaces.
The next review is due one interval after the last review, or after the validation date if no review
has been done yet. Syntra shows a review as "due soon" 60 days before the due date and as "overdue"
after it.

## 3 Scope of the review
The review covers at least:
- changes implemented since the last review and whether they were verified,
- incidents and deviations, including recurring problems,
- user accounts and role assignments, including leavers and segregation of duties,
- audit trail review results (SOP-ITO-005),
- backup and restore tests and disaster recovery readiness,
- open CAPAs, supplier status and the software support lifecycle.

## 4 Performing and recording the review
QA performs the review together with the System Owner and IT Operations. QA records the outcome in
Syntra with a summary of scope and findings and signs it electronically. Possible outcomes:
- The system remains in a validated state.
- Remediation is required: actions are raised as change requests or CAPAs with owners and due dates.
Periodic reviews can only be recorded for systems in the validated state.

## 5 Overdue reviews
An overdue periodic review must be reported to the Head of QA. The system may continue to be used,
but the review must be completed within 30 days of the due date or a documented risk assessment must
justify the delay.
