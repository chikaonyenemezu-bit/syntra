## 1 Purpose
This procedure defines how electronic signatures are executed in Syntra and how user accounts are
managed, in line with 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 §12 and §14.

## 2 Personal accounts
Every user has a personal account. Accounts and passwords must never be shared. Roles are assigned
on request of the line manager and approved by the System Owner; QA reviews role assignments during
the periodic review. Accounts of leavers are deactivated on their last working day.

## 3 Executing an electronic signature
Approvals, rejections and verifications of change requests and periodic reviews are electronic
signatures. To sign, the user re-enters their password and confirms the meaning of the signature,
for example "Approved (QA)". Signing with a password is legally binding and equivalent to a
handwritten signature. Users confirm this once in writing before their first signature.

## 4 Signature manifestation
Each signature shows the printed name of the signer, the user ID, the role, the date and time of
signing and the meaning of the signature. The signature is permanently linked to the record it signs
and cannot be copied, edited or deleted.

## 5 Failed signature attempts
If the password is wrong, nothing is signed and the failed attempt is recorded in the audit trail.
Repeated failed attempts on the same account must be reported to IT Security, as they may indicate
misuse of credentials.

## 6 Forgotten password
Users who forget their password request a reset from the IT Service Desk. The reset is performed
only after the identity of the user has been verified. A temporary password must be changed at the
next login.
